# Business Continuity and Incident Response Plan – Visa Inc.

## Objective
Develop a business continuity and incident response plan for a financial services organization to ensure continued operation during cybersecurity incidents.

## Tools Used
- Splunk SIEM
- Windows Event Logs
- Incident response procedures

## Skills Demonstrated
- Business continuity planning
- Incident response planning
- Risk assessment
- Security operations procedures

## Summary
This project demonstrates the ability to develop incident response and business continuity procedures for a financial organization. It includes identification of critical systems, threat scenarios, and recovery strategies to maintain secure financial operations.
